<!-- Slide comments
  Find a buddy:
    This way, you'll know at least one other person at the event.
  Enlist social media:
    Other attendees will see your post and the event organizers will promote your it as well — a built-in conversation starter!
    I got a job interview from a Twitter post at a conference.
  Nail down logistics:
    It's important to be honest with yourself when it comes to things like carpooling. Will you really want to spend an hour in the car with someone after a full day of networking?
  Sharing is preparing:
    Sign up to volunteer for the event ahead of time. It's a great way to meet people and you may get free entry or swag. If you don't have the bandwidth, bring things that people frequently need, like a power strip, phone charger, or mints.
-->

<!-- Slide comments
  Remembering a name:
    Whatever you have to do to get that name to stick, do it!
    Everyone forgets names. Just make a joke about it and then remember it.
    Make sure YOUR name tag is legible and front-facing.
  Including others:
    Some people are there with a significant other (but don't make an assumption).
    Some people get excited and talk over others. It's not that big of a deal unless it's repeatedly or on purpose – then something needs to be said.
    Teach Pac-Man
  Making the connection:
    Don't cling to the first person you meet or the only person you know like they're a lifeline – they may be too nice to tell you to bug off! If you're engaged in a good conversation, that's another story.
    When there's a lull, tell them it was nice to meet them, and if you felt a connection, as to connect on LinkedIn. Some people aren't active on the site and may not connect because of that (or some other reason). If so, just brush it off and move on.
    You can strike up a conversation with them later on in the event. Ask them how they've been enjoying it!
-->

You may want to...

- Arrive early to get a lay of the facilities
- Block out some time in the schedule to go to your car or hotel room and close your eyes
- Call someone for support
- Stay an extra night on your own dime for a work trip so you don't have to fly home directly from the training

All of these strategies are acceptable and understandable – especially to other introvert-leaning people!
